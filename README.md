# ToyBox

A little home for your work-in-progress songs. Bring songs in from Files, sort them into projects, play them on a tape-machine player, and record quick ideas.

## Put it on your iPhone

1. Open the ToyBox link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open ToyBox from your home screen.

Songs you add are saved on your phone only. They're never uploaded here.

## What's in this folder

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The screens: projects, a project's songs, record, player |
| `style.css` | The look: flat, retro, warm off-white with black controls |
| `app.js` | Everything that happens when you tap, play or record |
| `storage.js` | Saves your songs on your phone |
| `covers.js` | Paints the flat landscape cover for each project |
| `sw.js` | Keeps a copy of the app so it opens with no internet |
| `manifest.json`, `icons/` | The app's name and icon on your home screen |
